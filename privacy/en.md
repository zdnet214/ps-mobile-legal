Pixel Studio is an offline photo tool for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information, what it does not do, and the choices you have.

## 2. The short version

- The app works on your device. It does not upload your photos to us.
- The app does not create an account and does not include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
- The app does not request the Android `INTERNET` permission.
- Your photos, presets and language choice stay on your device unless you choose to share them or your device backup settings copy them.

## 3. What the app processes

The app processes the following on your device:

- **Photos you choose.** The app reads the photos you select so it can display, edit, split, merge or export them. It writes the results you save to your photo gallery.
- **App settings.** The app stores your language choice and your adjustment presets in its private application storage.
- **Technical image data.** Some files contain capture information such as ISO, exposure, aperture, focal length and colour profile. The app reads this to display the preview and to process the image correctly.

We do not receive copies of this information. It is processed locally by the app.

## 4. Permissions the app asks for

- **Photo library access.** Android grants the app access to your photos so it can show the library and save results. On Android 14 and later you can allow access to selected photos only. You can change or revoke this permission in system settings at any time.
- **No network permission.** The app does not declare the Android `INTERNET` permission. It cannot send your photos or usage data to a server.

If a future version needs a new permission, we will update this policy and the app's permission explanation before that version is released.

## 5. Why we process it and our legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of the service you request (Article 6(1)(b)).** Reading the photos you select and saving the results you ask for is the core of the app.
- **Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).** Keeping the app secure and reliable, and preventing abuse, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).** Where we must keep or disclose information to comply with the law.
- **Consent.** We do not use consent as the basis for the core local processing. If a future feature needs consent, it will ask separately.

Under the PIPL, we process the photos and settings you choose at your instruction to provide the app's photo-editing functions. We do not transfer your photos off your device.

## 6. Where data is stored and how long

- **Photos and results.** Photos stay in your gallery. Results stay there until you delete them. The app does not keep a second copy on a server.
- **Presets and language choice.** These stay in the app's private storage until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- **Android backup.** See section 8.

## 7. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your photos or app data. We do not operate a server that receives them, so there is no international transfer of your photos by us.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google processes installation, account and payment data under its own privacy policy. If you open the online version of this policy, the hosting provider processes the request as described in section 15.

## 8. Android system backup

Android can back up app data to your own Google account if you enable system backup. If you do, the operating system may include the app's private files, such as your language choice and adjustment presets, in that backup. The backup is controlled by your device and account settings. We do not receive it. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

Your photos and saved results are separate files in your gallery and are governed by your device's photo backup settings.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know whether we process your personal information and request a copy;
- correct inaccurate information;
- delete information;
- restrict or object to processing;
- receive your information in a portable format;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- complain to a data-protection authority.

Because we do not hold a server copy of your photos or app data, many requests can be fulfilled on your device: delete the relevant photos or results in your gallery, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. You can also contact us at **<CONTACT_EMAIL>** and we will explain what we can do.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. If you are in mainland China, you may exercise your PIPL rights, including access, correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent, by contacting us or using the device controls above. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

## 10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 where the local law sets a higher age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will address it.

## 11. Security

The app is designed to keep processing local. It uses Android's app sandbox and the photo permissions you grant. It does not transmit your photos to us. No device or storage method is completely secure, so keep your device updated, use a screen lock, and review which apps can access your photos.
